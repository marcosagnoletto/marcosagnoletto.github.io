I’m a mathematician moving into data and operational risk in finance. In my doctoral research, I’ve worked with coauthors to extend established results to situations where the usual methods no longer apply. That means identifying which assumptions are essential, finding another route when an argument breaks down, and explaining why each step supports the conclusion.

I want to bring that way of working to finance: understanding a process from beginning to end, tracing how information passes between stages, and identifying where a missing check or unreliable input can affect the outcome.
