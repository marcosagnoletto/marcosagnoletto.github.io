## Portfolio Risk — VaR & Expected Shortfall
 
Computation of Value-at-Risk and Expected Shortfall for a multi-asset portfolio using historical, parametric, and Monte Carlo approaches. Includes backtesting of the risk estimates against realized returns.
 
*Tools: Python, NumPy, pandas.*
